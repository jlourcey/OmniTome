## Full Description
*(4000 characters max — paste this as-is into Play Console)*

OmniTome is a premium e-reader for Android that connects directly to your personal Calibre Content Server, giving you seamless access to your entire book collection — wherever you are.

**Your library. Your server. Your privacy.**

Unlike cloud-dependent reading apps, OmniTome speaks to Calibre — the free, open-source library manager already trusted by millions of readers worldwide. Point OmniTome at your home server or a Calibre instance running anywhere on the internet, and your full collection syncs automatically. No third-party accounts. No subscription cloud. No one else's copy of your books.

**A reader built for serious book lovers**

OmniTome is designed for people who care about how they read, not just what they read. The reading experience is clean, focused, and fully customizable — choose your font, size, line spacing, margins, and theme. Read in light mode for daytime sessions or switch to the dark navy-and-gold theme for late-night reading that won't strain your eyes.

**Everything you need, nothing you don't**

• Sync your entire Calibre library over your home network or remotely
• Read EPUB and PDF files with a smooth, native reading experience
• Browse and download directly from your Calibre Content Server via OPDS
• Import local EPUB and PDF files from your device or Google Drive
• Upload local books directly to Calibre from the app
• Full metadata support: titles, authors, series, tags, covers, and descriptions
• Highlight passages in four colors and add typed annotations
• Create bookmarks and navigate chapters at a glance
• Export your highlights and notes as a formatted PDF
• Tablet-optimized two-page reading layout
• Series grouping and multi-field search with tag filtering
• Favorites shelf for quick access to books you're actively reading

**Built on trusted open standards**

OmniTome's reading engine is built on the Readium SDK — the same foundation used by major publishing platforms — delivering reliable, standards-compliant EPUB rendering. Your book files stay on your device and your server; OmniTome never uploads or transmits them anywhere else.

**One-time purchase. No subscriptions. No ads.**

Pay once and use OmniTome forever. There are no recurring fees, no in-app purchases, no advertisements, and no premium tiers. Everything described above is included in a single purchase.

**Requirements**

• A Calibre Content Server accessible on your local network or internet (Calibre is free software available at calibre-ebook.com)
• Android 8.0 or later
