About OmniTome Media

OmniTome Media is an independent software company built around a single belief: that serious readers deserve tools that respect their libraries, their privacy, and their time.

We build reading software for people who have invested years in building a personal book collection — and who want to take that collection with them without handing it over to a subscription platform or a third-party cloud. Our tools connect to the software readers already use and trust, like Calibre, and make that library feel native on every device.

We don't run advertising. We don't track usage. We don't sell data. We sell software — once — and then get out of the way so you can read.

OmniTome

OmniTome is our flagship Android application — a full-featured e-reader that connects directly to your personal Calibre Content Server. Browse your entire library, download books, read EPUB and PDF files, highlight and annotate passages, track your reading progress, and sync everything back to Calibre — all without your books ever touching anyone else's server.

Key features include:

  • Direct OPDS sync with your Calibre Content Server over your home network or remotely
  • Full EPUB and PDF reading with customizable fonts, sizing, and themes
  • Highlights and annotations in four colors, with typed notes and PDF export
  • Series grouping, tag filtering, and multi-field search across your entire library
  • Local file import from device storage or Google Drive
  • One-tap upload of local books back to your Calibre library
  • Tablet-optimized two-page reading layout
  • No subscription. No ads. One purchase, forever.

OmniTome is available on the Google Play Store.
